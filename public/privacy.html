隐私政策 Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 12 日 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

我们收集什么 What we collect

只有你主动提供的信息：表单里填写的邮箱，以及你自愿提交的体检报告（Labcorp / Quest 等英文报告）。除此之外，本站不收集其他个人信息。

We only collect information you provide voluntarily: the email address you enter in our forms, and the lab report you choose to send us (e.g. Labcorp / Quest). We do not collect any other personal information.

我们怎么用 How we use it

你的邮箱只用于联系你、交付解读；你的体检报告只用于本次解读，不用于任何其他用途。解读由人工＋AI 辅助完成，每一份都会经人工复核后再发出。

Your email is used only to contact you and deliver your interpretation. Your report is used only for that one interpretation, and for nothing else. Interpretations are produced with human review and AI assistance, and every one is checked by a human before delivery.

去标识指引 De-identification

发送报告前，你可以把姓名、出生日期、地址等个人信息涂掉，只保留检查项目和数值。我们会在联系你时附上这份指引。

Before sending your report, you may black out your name, date of birth, address and other identifying details, keeping only the test items and values. We will include these instructions when we contact you.

不出售、不共享 No sale or sharing

我们不出售、不出租你的邮箱与报告，也不会与任何第三方共享。你的报告不会用于广告、训练或任何与本次解读无关的用途。

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address or report with any third party. Your report is never used for advertising, model training, or any purpose unrelated to your interpretation.

删除 Deletion

解读交付后，你可以随时要求删除你的报告原件与解读内容。写邮件到 kefu@yllife.vip，注明提交时用的邮箱，我们收到后会删除并不再保留。

After delivery, you may ask us to delete your report and interpretation at any time. Email kefu@yllife.vip from the address you submitted with, and we will delete them and keep no copy.

不追踪 No tracking

本站不安装任何广告追踪像素与分析脚本。验证期的统计只靠表单提交时记录的渠道来源（?src= 参数）。

This site installs no advertising tracking pixels or analytics scripts. During the validation period, the only statistics we keep are the referral source recorded when you submit a form (the ?src= parameter).

为父母提交 Submitting for a parent

如果你是替父母提交报告，请先征得父母本人的同意。

If you are submitting a report on behalf of a parent, please get their consent first.

联系我们 Contact

对本政策或你的数据有任何问题，写邮件到 kefu@yllife.vip。

Questions about this policy or your data: kefu@yllife.vip.
